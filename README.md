# Gridiron Kingdom

A college football dynasty simulation for iOS, built with Flutter and Dart.

Created by **Owen Degnan**, a Computer Science student at Saint Francis University, under **Game Day Studios**.

## About the Game

Gridiron Kingdom puts players in charge of building a college football program. Recruit players, manage your roster, develop your coaching career, and compete across multiple seasons.

## Features

* FBS and FCS programs with non-conference matchups between divisions
* Recruiting, player development, and transfer portal decisions
* Roster retention through NIL
* Coaching contracts and job opportunities
* Offensive and defensive schemes with system mastery
* Conference movement and opportunities to progress from FCS to FBS
* Season rankings, awards, and postseason competition

## Technology

* **Flutter** for the application interface
* **Dart** for gameplay and simulation logic
* **Xcode** for iOS builds and distribution
* **Git and GitHub** for version control

## Development

I built Gridiron Kingdom around my interest in sports and software development, taking the project from a game concept to an iOS App Store release. I continue to expand its features, improve simulation balance, and incorporate player feedback.
